System Overview & Cloud Architecture

The Church Financial Management and Membership System (CFMMS) is an enterprise web platform specifically engineered for church administrations to maintain transparent, audited financial records and accurate member stewardship directories.

🔒 Serverless Postgres

Encrypted relational cloud database with point-in-time recovery and automated backups.

🛡️ Role-Based Guard

Strict separation of duties prevents fraudulent self-verifications and unauthorized edits.

⚡ Real-Time Engine

Cross-tab broadcast channels and 15s background auto-sync keep financial KPIs synchronized.

Executive Financial Dashboard & Live Sync

The Dashboard (index.html) is the central nerve center of the church finance platform:

  • 5 Executive Metric Cards (KPIs): Monitors Total Membership, Monthly Inflows, Operating Disbursements, Net Operating Cashflow (Surplus/Deficit), and Allocated Reserves (Pastor's Personal Savings + District Apportionment).
  • Glow Highlight on Update: When a user enters data in another room or tab, the dashboard KPI numbers pulse green to highlight changes.
  • 4 Real-Time Analytics Charts: 6-Month Cashflow trend line, Fund Volume Share doughnut, Payment Channel breakdown, and Demographics active ratio.
  • Live Synchronization: Background auto-polling updates the metrics every 15 seconds without requiring a manual page refresh.

Collections Management & Batch Verification

Collections represent all Sunday Tithes, General Offerings, Pledges, Building Funds, and Love Gifts received by the church.

Step 1: Recording a Collection
  1. Click "+ Record Collection" or press Ctrl + Enter.
  2. Search and select the member name (or choose "Guest").
  3. Enter the total cash or check amount. The system automatically computes deductions (e.g. 10% Pastor's Savings, 10% Apportionment, 80% Net General Fund).
  4. Click "Save Collection". If entered by a Secretary, status defaults to Pending (For Review).
Step 2: Admin Batch Verification & Secretary Edit Locks
  1. Admin navigates to the "Pending Review" status tab.
  2. Select individual checkboxes or click the Header Checkbox to select all pending records.
  3. The Batch Verification Toolbar expands above the table showing the Total Reconciled Amount.
  4. Cross-check against the physical counting sheet and click "✓ Verify Selected (N)". All items transition to Verified instantly.
  5. Secretary Lock Rule: Secretaries may edit collection records while status is Pending. Once Verified, records become locked from Secretary edits.
Step 3: Sunday Cash Count & Reconciliation Tally Statement
  1. Click "💵 Sunday Cash Tally" button on the Collections page or Financial Reports section.
  2. Enter currency denomination counts (₱1,000, ₱500, ₱200, ₱100, ₱50, ₱20 banknotes, coins, loose coins, checks, and GCash/online total).
  3. The system automatically reconciles physical cash vs. envelope ledger totals:
    • TALLY_MATCH: Physical cash count exactly matches envelope ledger totals.
    • VARIANCE_DETECTED: Discrepancy detected; requires entering a mandatory audit note.
  4. Select official signatories from strict role-filtered dropdowns:
    • Counter / Steward: Strictly users with role Secretary.
    • Finance Secretary: Strictly users with role Secretary.
    • Treasurer / Admin: Strictly users with role Treasurer or Admin.
  5. Click "Save Cash Count & Reconciliation Statement". The statement is automatically included as Sheet 4 in Excel exports!

Expense Disbursements & Voucher Approvals

All church expenditures (Utilities, Pastor Honorarium, Ministry Outreaches, Building Maintenance) must be documented through sequential Expense Vouchers.

Filing an Expense Voucher

Specify Payee Name, Account Category, Disbursement Method (Cash, Check #, Bank Transfer), and attached official receipt description.

Audited Void Workflow

Vouchers cannot simply be deleted. Voiding requires entering an official audit reason (e.g., "Duplicate check issued") which is permanently logged in the Audit Trail.

Membership Directory & Stewardship Records

The Membership Module (members.html) tracks all church adherents, contact directories, family relationships, and individual contribution histories.

🌟 Key Membership Features:
  • Standardized Name Capitalization: Auto-formats into FIRST M.I. LAST (e.g. JUAN D. DELA CRUZ) for clean government & financial reporting.
  • Nicknames & Aliases Linking: Map informal donor aliases (e.g. "Kuya Johnny" $\rightarrow$ JONARD SANTOCILDES) so collection auto-complete matches immediately.
  • Family Linkages: Group households together (Head of Household, Spouse, Children).
  • Individual Stewardship History: Click "View Ledger" on any member to see their entire giving journey across all years.

Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

Q: A Secretary recorded a wrong amount that is already verified. How to correct?

Only an Admin has the authority to edit or re-verify locked records. The Admin can open the Edit Collection modal, adjust the figure, and save. All corrections are permanently audited with the Admin's timestamp.

Q: New collections entered on another computer are not showing immediately.

The Real-Time Sync engine automatically pulls changes every 15 seconds. You can also click the "Sync Now" button at the top right to force an instant database refresh.

Q: How to reset forgotten passwords?

An Admin can navigate to Settings $\rightarrow$ User Management to securely assign a temporary password for any church staff member.
